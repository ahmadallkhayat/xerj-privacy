XERJ Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

In short

  • Your wallets, transactions and debts are stored only on your phone.
  • XERJ has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • The only internet connection XERJ makes is to read the public US dollar exchange rate.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint checks are done by your phone. XERJ never sees your biometric data.

Who we are

XERJ is a personal finance app for tracking spending, balances and debts in Iraqi dinar and US dollars. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. Questions are welcome at the contact address at the end of this page.

Information you enter

You can record wallets and their balances, income and expenses with categories, notes and dates, currency exchanges between wallets, and debts, including the names of people you lend to or borrow from.

All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no way to see it.

Information we collect

None. XERJ does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. There is no sign-up, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Exchange rate

When the exchange rate is set to Live (the default), XERJ reads the public US dollar price from Hatwan Exchange (hatwanexchange.com). This happens when the app opens, when you return to it, about every 30 minutes while it is open, and when you pull down to refresh.

None of your financial information is included in this request. Like any website visit, Hatwan Exchange's server can see technical details such as your device's IP address. Their handling of that information is covered by their own policies.

If you set the rate to Manual in Settings, XERJ makes no internet connections at all.

App lock and biometrics

If you turn on the app lock, XERJ asks your phone's operating system to verify you with Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, face unlock or your device passcode. The check happens entirely on your phone. XERJ only receives whether it succeeded and never has access to your face, fingerprint or passcode.

While the lock is on, XERJ asks your phone to hide its contents in the app switcher. On Android it also blocks screenshots of the app.

Backups

Backups happen only when you choose to make one. Export backup creates a file containing all of your XERJ data and opens your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes, for example your files, cloud storage or a message. Restore backup reads only the file you select.

Backup files are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere private, because anyone with the file can read its contents.

Permissions

  • Face ID (iOS): used only for the optional app lock.
  • Files: XERJ opens only the backup file you pick when restoring.

XERJ does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or notifications.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app. Because your data is never sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

XERJ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
